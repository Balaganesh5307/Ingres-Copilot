Ingres Copilot: AI Groundwater Intelligence Assistant
AI-Powered Groundwater Intelligence Assistant for INGRES

SIH25066 – Ministry of Jal Shakti

1. Executive Summary

Ingres Copilot is an AI-powered virtual assistant designed for the India Ground Water Resource Estimation System (INGRES). The platform enables users to access groundwater assessment data, historical records, technical reports, guidelines, and scientific insights through natural language conversations instead of manually searching through large documents and datasets.

The platform combines:

Large Language Models (LLMs)
Retrieval Augmented Generation (RAG)
Groundwater Knowledge Intelligence
Interactive Analytics
Explainable AI
Source-Based Responses

to create an intelligent decision-support ecosystem for government officials, hydrogeologists, researchers, planners, policymakers, students, and citizens.

2. Problem Statement

The Ministry of Jal Shakti publishes groundwater assessment data and reports through INGRES. The platform contains extensive groundwater-related information, including assessment reports, historical datasets, technical manuals, and resource statistics.

However, users face several challenges:

Large volume of groundwater reports
Complex hydrogeological terminology
Difficulty accessing historical records
Time-consuming manual searches
Lack of conversational access to information
Limited accessibility for non-technical users
Dependence on support personnel

As a result, valuable groundwater information remains underutilized and difficult to access efficiently.

3. Proposed Solution

Develop an AI-powered virtual assistant capable of:

Understanding natural language questions
Retrieving groundwater information from INGRES
Answering queries with source citations
Generating summaries from technical reports
Visualizing groundwater trends and statistics
Explaining complex groundwater concepts in simple language
Assisting users with intelligent data exploration

The platform transforms INGRES from a static repository into an intelligent groundwater knowledge ecosystem.
